Chimichurri Chicken Grain Bowls (Meal Prep)

Roasted chicken thighs over brown rice and broccoli, finished with a bright store-bought chimichurri sauce added day-of.

INGREDIENTS
• 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs
• 3 tbsp olive oil
• 1 tsp kosher salt
• 8 oz Marketside chimichurri sauce paste
• 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
• 1 1/2 cup bagged brown rice
• 1 1/2 tsp Better Than Bouillon chicken base
• 2 frozen steamable broccoli

STEPS
1. In a small jar, stir together about two-thirds of the 8 oz Marketside chimichurri sauce paste, 2 tbsp of the 3 tbsp olive oil, and the 1 tbsp red wine vinegar. This loosens the paste into a spoonable finishing sauce. Set aside — this is your day-of sauce. Keep the remaining third of the paste separate for the rub.
2. Pat the 1 1/2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels — dry surface is what gets you a real sear. Toss with the remaining 1 tbsp 3 tbsp olive oil, the 1 tsp kosher salt, and the reserved third of the 8 oz Marketside chimichurri sauce paste, rubbing it in to coat.
3. Roast at 425°F on a foil-lined sheet until the thickest thigh hits 165-170°F, about 18-22 minutes. ⏱ 20m
4. Switch to broil for 2-3 minutes to char the edges. Carryover will bring the dark meat to that ideal tender texture. Rest 5 minutes, then slice or chop. ⏱ 3m
5. Cook the 1 1/2 cup bagged brown rice per package directions, stirring the 1 1/2 tsp Better Than Bouillon chicken base into the cooking water so the rice picks up savory depth throughout. ⏱ 25m
6. Microwave the 2 frozen steamable broccoli about 1 minute short of the package time so it stays firm — it'll finish cooking when you reheat portions later. ⏱ 4m
7. Divide rice, broccoli, and chicken into 4 containers. Store the chimichurri sauce separately.
8. Reheat a bowl, then spoon the chimichurri over everything right before eating. Adding it fresh keeps the herbs and acid bright instead of dulling them in storage.

NOTES
Keeping most of the chimichurri as a day-of finish is what preserves the punch — the paste's flavor fades if it sits cooked or stored for days. The thinned sauce keeps about a week in the fridge. If you'd rather swap in frozen salmon: roast at 400°F for 12-15 min instead, and go easy on the rub since salmon takes the sauce well on its own.
