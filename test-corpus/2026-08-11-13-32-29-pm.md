Creamy Leek-Braised Whole Chicken with Torn-Bread Stuffing

A whole chicken braised over leeks in white wine, cream, and butter — with Dijon, garlic, and bouillon for depth, fennel seed standing in for tarragon, and lemon to keep all that richness bright. The bread soaks up the drippings at the end.

INGREDIENTS
• 1 whole chicken (about 3.5-4 lb)
• 3 leeks, white and light-green parts, cut into 1-inch chunks
• 3 tbsp unsalted butter
• 5 fresh thyme sprigs
• 1/2 tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed
• 3/4 cup dry white wine
• 1 1/2 tbsp jarred minced garlic
• 1 tsp Better Than Bouillon chicken base
• 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
• 1 cup heavy cream
• 1 tbsp lemon juice
• 2 tsp kosher salt
• 1/2 tsp black pepper
• 5 cup crusty bread, torn into large chunks
• 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped
• 1 lemon, zested (for finishing)

STEPS
1. Heat oven to 400°F. Pat the 1 whole chicken (about 3.5-4 lb) very dry inside and out with paper towels — the drier it is, the better the skin holds up in the braise.
2. Scatter 3 leeks, white and light-green parts, cut into 1-inch chunks in a baking dish. Dot with 3 tbsp unsalted butter, tuck in 5 fresh thyme sprigs, and sprinkle over 1/2 tsp fennel seeds, lightly crushed and about half the 2 tsp kosher salt.
3. In a bowl or measuring cup, whisk 3/4 cup dry white wine with 1 1/2 tbsp jarred minced garlic, 1 tsp Better Than Bouillon chicken base, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, and 1 tbsp lemon juice until the bouillon and mustard dissolve. Pour over the leeks.
4. Season the 1 whole chicken (about 3.5-4 lb) all over with the remaining 2 tsp kosher salt and the 1/2 tsp black pepper. Set it breast-up on the leeks, then pour 1 cup heavy cream over the top and around the bird so it pools in the dish.
5. Bake at 400°F, basting the bird with the pan cream every 15 minutes. Start checking temperature at the 1-hour mark — you want 165°F in the thickest part of the breast and 175°F in the thigh. Depending on size this usually takes 70-90 minutes total. ⏱ 1h
6. Transfer the chicken to a board or plate and let it rest, loosely tented, while you do the stuffing. Fish out and discard the 5 fresh thyme sprigs sprigs from the dish. ⏱ 10m
7. Stir the 5 cup crusty bread, torn into large chunks into the leek-and-cream mixture, pressing so the bread soaks up the drippings. Return the dish to the 400°F oven until the bread is warmed through and the exposed edges crisp, about 10-15 minutes. ⏱ 12m
8. Let the stuffing rest a few minutes. Carve the chicken. Top the stuffing (and the chicken) with 1/4 cup fresh parsley, chopped and the 1 lemon, zested (for finishing), and add a final squeeze of lemon if it needs more brightness. Taste and adjust salt.

NOTES
Fennel seed covers the anise note tarragon would have brought — crush the seeds lightly so it releases without being crunchy. Because you're not dry-brining, patting the bird really dry in step 1 matters most for skin. The cream will look broken/curdled after braising — that's normal and it tastes great; just stir it into the bread. If the stuffing is swimming, spoon off a little liquid before adding bread; if it's dry, the cream sauce is your friend. Zest raw at the very end for the brightest citrus aroma.
